Prescription Safety & Patient Guidelines

MediFind promotes responsible pharmaceutical distribution in compliance with the Drugs and Cosmetics Act.

  • Schedule H & X Medications: A valid physical prescription issued by a registered medical practitioner is mandatory upon pickup at the dispensing pharmacy.
  • Stock Freshness Verification: Inventory timestamps reflect the latest pharmacy management upload. In-stock badges indicate reported store availability, not guaranteed physical holds.
  • Emergency Care: For severe medical trauma, acute symptoms, or life-threatening emergencies, immediately call emergency triage services or visit the nearest hospital emergency room.
